## Shared context (prepend to every prompt)
Ultra high-end, Awwwards Site of the Day calibre portfolio website for Tina Campbell, a fashion and portrait photographer in Edmonton, Alberta. Design system "Runway + Editorial": deep ink black background #0C0C0F, photographs glowing on black like lit catwalk frames, white type, crimson #B3261E as the ONLY accent and the only button colour (pill buttons), razor-thin hairline rules, generous negative space, subtle film grain. Type: tall high-contrast fashion serif display (Didone / Instrument Serif feel) for big words, clean grotesk for small UI, tiny monospace captions with frame numbers like "No. 014" and Edmonton locations. Film-strip details (sprocket holes, frame numbers, timecode) used sparingly as accents. Her photos (attached references) must look like her real work: model in a crimson ball gown on a vintage teal 1960s Fargo pickup under hard blue sky; model in black satin gown on the Alberta Legislature steps and under an ornate black lamp post against saturated blue sky; Walterdale Bridge white arch at golden hour; runway looks under magenta stage light; model in red against white snow; glam portraits; bridal; couples. Use her real logo exactly as in the attached reference: the hand-lettered dry-brush script "TCPhoto" with a tall slashing T stem, heavy textured C and P, small thin script "HOTO", in white on ink. Never redraw it as a typeface. Header on every page: TCPhoto logo left; nav "Work  Fashion & Editorial  About  Services  Journal" in small grotesk; crimson pill "Book a shoot" right. All words spelled correctly, minimal text, crisp and legible. Real website screenshot look, not a poster, not a device-on-desk photo, no watermarks, no captions about the image itself.

# Motion videos: Grok Imagine (reference_to_video), animating the actual layouts
Each clip is a preview of one effect we will build. 16:9, 720p, 6 s, no audio. Staged frames are image_edit crops/extensions of the approved layouts, so the video starts from the real design.
Motion language: each prompt is written as a camera or object move with explicit start and end positions (video models need positions, not verbs).

## Prompts
V1: "Screen recording of a luxury dark photography website scrolling smoothly. Start: the hero fills the frame (photo of model in black satin gown under ornate lamp post, white serif TINA CAMPBELL). Over 6 seconds the whole page moves upward by one full screen height with a soft ease-out: the hero exits through the top edge while a black section with a row of five tall fashion photos rises from the bottom edge and settles in the centre. Flat screen capture, no camera shake, no 3D tilt, text stays crisp and unchanged."
V2: "Screen recording of a website gallery. Start: a row of tall runway photographs in a film strip on black. Over 6 seconds the whole row slides from right to left by exactly two photo widths at constant speed with a gentle ease at the end; the photo arriving at the centre grows 15 percent larger and becomes sharp and bright while photos at the edges shrink, darken and blur. The crimson progress dot on the thin line moves from left third to the middle. Flat screen capture, header and logo stay fixed."
V3: "Screen recording of a hover effect. Start: all photos black and white except red dresses. A crimson circular cursor glides from the left edge to the centre photo in the first second; at contact, concentric liquid ripples spread across that photo from the cursor point and colour floods outward from the ripple centre until the whole photo is full colour with a faint red-cyan fringe at its edges; the ripples settle flat by second 5. Other photos stay black and white and do not move. Flat screen capture."
V4: "Screen recording of an editorial page loading. Start frame: black left panel empty, right photo column hidden behind black. Second 0 to 2: the serif headline Fashion & Editorial rises line by line from below hidden masks into place. Second 1 to 4: the tall photo on the right is revealed by a black mask sliding down from top to bottom. Second 3 to 5: the numbered series list appears one row at a time, the first row in crimson. End frame equals the finished page. Flat, no camera move."
V5: "Screen recording of a page transition. Start: a grid of photo tiles on black. The red gown on teal truck tile expands smoothly from its grid position to fill the whole screen as a full-bleed hero over 3 seconds, while the surrounding tiles slide off to the left like film frames advancing with slight motion blur; a thin crimson line wipes across. End frame equals the series cover page. Flat screen capture."
V6: "Parallax depth in a website hero. Three layers: far city skyline at dusk, middle white arch bridge, foreground photographer seen from behind holding a camera. Over 6 seconds the view moves downward as if scrolling: the foreground person moves up out of frame by a full third of the screen height, the bridge moves up by about one sixth, the skyline moves up only slightly; the white serif name TINA CAMPBELL stays between the bridge and the person and does not change. Smooth, flat, no camera rotation."
